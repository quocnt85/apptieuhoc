# Feature Spec: NovaStar Companion Collection & Nurturing System

## Purpose
Defines the mechanics, leveling curves, companion customization closet, and companion interaction behaviors in NovaStars.

## Feature Architecture
Learners adopt and nurture a NovaStar Companion (e.g. Orion the Fox, Astra the Star-Bunny). Companions serve as non-judgmental learning partners during quests.

## Companion Features
1. **Interactive Companion Hub**: Feed, pet, and customize companions using earned Star Shards.
2. **Quest Scaffolding Partner**: Companions pop up during lessons to provide scaffolded hints and celebrate correct answers.
3. **Mastery Evolution**: Reaching Companion Level 5 and 10 unlocks visual evolution effects (Star Aura, Crown).
