SECTION 01

Lesson Design Philosophy

NLAS operationalizes 11 Immutable Design Principles into strict system constraints, educational rationales, trade-offs, risks, and concrete examples.

1. Competency before Content
Focus strictly on 1 primary competency per lesson. Zero passive lesson bloat.
LSCAF MappedFocus
2. Experience before Explanation
Constructivist mental models built before introducing formal terms or vocabulary.
PiagetianIntuitive
3. Play before Teaching
First 60 seconds are pure gameplay interactions with zero instructional text.
Intrinsic Motivation
4. Discovery before Instruction
Child tests at least 2 hypotheses before NPC scaffold hints appear.
Inductive Learning
5. Learning by Doing
Max passive screen time: 15s (G1-2) / 25s (G3-5). Continuous active trial.
Kolb Cycle
6. Positive Reinforcement
100% Growth Mindset feedback targeting effort and strategy choice.
Dweck Theory
7. Safe Failure
No "Game Over" states. Failure routes to constructive Scaffolding Debug Loops.
Zero Penalties
8. Mastery Learning
Requires ≥ 80% Boss accuracy + Metacognitive Reflection to complete.
Bloom Mastery
9. Behavior Change
Every lesson dispatches a Real-Life Mission to the Parent App for home transfer.
Fogg Model
10. Reflection before Completion
Reward locked behind articulating strategy and decision reasoning.
Metacognition
11. Real-Life Transfer
Explicit bridge connecting game mechanics to daily home and school situations.
Bridge Building
SECTION 02

Universal 7-Stage Lesson Architecture

Every NovaStars lesson follows a universal 7-stage sequence, balancing structural rigor with thematic freedom.

STAGE 1

Narrative Hook & Discovery

Establish emotional stakes, introduce adventure context, trigger natural curiosity.

⏱ 60 - 90sInput: World State
Engage & Quest
STAGE 2

Experiential Inquiry (Play & Discover)

Unguided exploration where child tests intuitive hypotheses in problem space.

⏱ 120 - 180sInput: Mini Game
Cognitive Conflict
STAGE 3

Guided Practice & Scaffolding

Targeted scaffolding, strategy modeling, and progressive hint fading.

⏱ 180 - 240sInput: Diagnostic Data
Scaffolded Trial
STAGE 4

Mastery Boss Challenge

High-stakes, unassisted evaluation of primary competency in narrative climax.

⏱ 180 - 300sInput: Boss Pattern
≥ 80% Mastery Check
STAGE 5

Metacognitive Reflection

Articulate why strategy worked, self-assess performance, consolidate memory.

⏱ 90 - 120sInput: Choice History
Metacognition
STAGE 6

Real-World Action Bridge (Parent Transfer)

Translate game mechanic to home behavior; send Real-Life Mission to Parent App.

⏱ 60 - 90sInput: Mission Card
Parent App Sync
STAGE 7

Mastery Certification & Reward

Award competency tokens, update character progression, unlock map paths.

⏱ 45 - 60sInput: Badge Metadata
Mastery Unlocked

SECTION 09

6-Layer Validation Protocol

Layer 1: Educational
Pedagogical integrity & LSCAF skill alignment audit.
Layer 2: Trauma & Safety
Psychological safety, zero shame, safe failure verification.
Layer 3: Age & Lexicon
Vocabulary check against G1-5 Lexile readability targets.
Layer 4: Bias & Inclusion
Gender, socio-economic, and cultural non-stereotyping check.
Layer 5: Consistency
UI mechanic & character lore adherence across modules.
