# NovaStars Foundational Hypotheses & Strategic Assumptions

## Purpose
Defines the core underlying hypotheses, market assumptions, target user behaviors, and strategic constraints that validate the NovaStars product design.

## Scope
Governs product strategy validation, market positioning, and risk mitigation across all domains.

## Strategic Hypotheses

### Hypothesis 1: Intrinsic Gamification Drives Sustained Life Skill Learning
- **Assumption:** Children aged 6–12 will spend 3x more time engaging with life skills (e.g. financial management, emotional regulation) if learning is embedded within narrative mini-game loops rather than traditional quizzes.
- **Validation Metric:** D30 retention >45% and average daily completion of 2+ quest nodes.

### Hypothesis 2: Parent Visibility Without Intrusion Increases Household Lifetime Value (LTV)
- **Assumption:** Parents willingly pay for subscription access if provided with real-time mastery insights and actionable offline family discussion prompts that bridge digital learning to household habits.
- **Validation Metric:** Parent portal activation rate >70% and offline activity completion rate >40%.

### Hypothesis 3: Multi-Agent AI Pipelines Lower Content Production Costs By 90%
- **Assumption:** Specialized multi-agent LLM pipelines governed by strict JSON schemas and 5 Quality Review Gates can produce 10,000+ high-quality lessons at <$0.50/lesson cost.
- **Validation Metric:** >95% first-pass pass rate at Gate 1-4 and human reviewer throughput of >20 lessons/hour at Gate 5.
