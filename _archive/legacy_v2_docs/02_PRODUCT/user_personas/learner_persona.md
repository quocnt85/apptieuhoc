# Primary Learner Persona: Leo (Age 8, Grade 3)

## Purpose
Defines the primary learner target demographic, cognitive abilities, emotional drivers, and design constraints for the NovaStars mobile application.

## Profile Summary
- **Age:** 8 years old (Grade 3 Primary School)
- **Tech Literacy:** High touch-screen familiarity (plays mobile games, watches YouTube Kids)
- **Attention Span:** 5 to 8 minutes per session
- **Reading Ability:** Can read short sentences (max 15-20 words), prefers visual cues and voiceovers.

## Goals & Motivations
- Collect cool NovaStar companions and unlock shiny costumes.
- Complete exciting story quests and defeat boss monsters.
- Feel proud showing achievements to parents.

## UX Design Rules for Leo
1. **Large Touch Targets**: Minimum 48x48 dp button sizes.
2. **Audio Voiceovers**: All dialogue text MUST include voiceover play buttons.
3. **No Penalty for Failure**: Instant retry with friendly companion encouragement.
