# NovaStars Strategic Objectives & Target OKRs

## Purpose
Defines the key quantitative and qualitative objectives, key results (OKRs), and success metrics guiding product development and content production.

## Core OKRs (2026–2027)

### Objective 1: Deliver High-Impact Life Skill Competency Curriculum
- **KR 1.1:** Author and freeze 10,000+ atomic competency-based learning objects across 5 primary domains.
- **KR 1.2:** Achieve >85% verified mastery rate among active primary school learners.

### Objective 2: Scale AI Production System (AIPS) Efficiently
- **KR 2.1:** Maintain >95% first-pass review rate for AI-generated learning experiences through 5 Quality Review Gates.
- **KR 2.2:** Reduce content generation cost per lesson to <$0.50 while maintaining 100% human-in-the-loop QA.

### Objective 3: Drive High Learner Retention & Intrinsic Engagement
- **KR 3.1:** Achieve D30 retention >45% powered by the NovaStars Fun Framework and companion progression.
- **KR 3.2:** Maintain average session completion rate >90%.
